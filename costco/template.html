Costco recall questions, answered

How do I get a refund for a recalled Costco item?

Bring the item — or just your membership card — to any Costco returns desk. Costco's membership system tracks your purchases, so refunds on recalled items are typically issued without a receipt.

Does Costco notify members about recalls?

Often, yes — Costco is unusual in that it can email or mail members who bought a recalled item, because purchases are tied to membership. But notices can lag or miss you, which is why a second set of eyes helps.

Where does this data come from?

Directly from the U.S. FDA's public enforcement database, refreshed daily. Always confirm details against the official notice at fda.gov before acting.
